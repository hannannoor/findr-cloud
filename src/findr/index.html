Built-in trust

Anyone can say it’s theirs.
Findr actually checks.

When someone hands in a found item, they jot down a couple of private details about it — things you can only know if it’s really yours.

Kept off the listing

What’s inside it, a scratch, a sticker — recorded privately and never shown on the item page or sent to your browser.

Turned into questions by AI

Amazon Bedrock writes questions from those details, marks your answers against them, and scores how well they line up.

The finder always makes the final call — they get your score either way. Findr advises, it doesn’t hand your item over automatically.
